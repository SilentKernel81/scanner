Camera

Drag a rectangle around the box to use only that part of the picture, so every camera shows the box the same way. A Raspberry Pi camera reads out just that part of its sensor, at its full resolution; a USB camera sends the whole picture and the station cuts it. The picture is shown as the camera sends it; turning it is done on the server's page. After changing the crop, draw the fixed area again if you use one.

Drag inside the green rectangle to move it, right-click the picture to clear it. The shape only decides what you can drag. It changes how the camera's live view looks, not how cards are found or what a capture is: the card is cut from the full picture by its own outline, at the same size with or without a crop.
