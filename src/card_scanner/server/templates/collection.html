Trades

Cards set aside for a trade stay in your collection, marked, until you confirm the trade - only then are they removed. To start one, tick cards on the Inventory tab and press Set aside for trade. A trade can also list cards you would receive from someone's imported collection (below); those are only a record - confirming adds nothing to your collection.

Find cards to trade or sell

Lists the cards that none of your decks uses and that EDHREC does not list for the commander of any of your decks. Needs the internet: it asks EDHREC about each deck with a commander (a moment the first time, then from a 7-day cache). Cards that a trade already holds are not counted.

Other people's collections

Import someone else's collection file (this app's export, Moxfield or ManaBox) under their name to browse it. From there, Receive puts a card in a trade as something you would get, and Buy puts it on your buy list. Nothing here is part of your collection or its backups - delete an import when you are done; what a trade or the buy list took from it stays.
